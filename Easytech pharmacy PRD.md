# EasyTech Pharmacy Limited - PRD V1

## Project Name
EasyTech Pharmacy Limited Online Pharmacy Store (Version 1)

## Project Overview
EasyTech Pharmacy Limited wants to build a simple and modern online pharmacy website where customers can browse pharmaceutical products, view product details, and contact the pharmacy to place orders.

This is Version 1 (MVP) and will focus on showcasing products professionally while providing customers with an easy way to contact the pharmacy.

## Business Goal
To create an online presence that allows customers to:
- Browse available pharmaceutical products
- Learn about EasyTech Pharmacy Limited
- Contact the pharmacy easily
- Increase product visibility
- Improve customer convenience

## Mission Statement
Making people happier, healthier, and helping them live longer.
